Welcome to StickLab

Build a little world. Watch its physics. Then see what happens when you interfere.

  • Grab: drag figures, objects or empty space to pan.
  • Build: place boxes, balls, anchors and articulated stick figures.
  • Connect: tap two objects with Rope or Spring selected. Anchors make fixed suspension points.
  • Live: place a Resident to explore, investigate, avoid hazards and help with experiments. Select one using Grab to take over, then Release to resume its decisions.
  • Play: tap a stick figure in Grab mode; move with A/D or arrows, jump with W/↑/space. On touch, use the on-screen buttons.
  • Tools: keys 1–8 switch tools. Scroll to zoom. Space pauses when no figure is being controlled.
  • Game Modes: use the visible 🎮 Games button to choose Sandbox, Physics Puzzles, Engineering, Ragdoll Chaos or Living World. These are different starting worlds with the same physics tools.
  • Optional levels: choose Physics Puzzles, then pick a level. Move the blue ball into the green goal, then return to your preserved sandbox whenever you like.
  • Keep it: Save/Load use device storage; Export/Import transfer scenes between devices.
