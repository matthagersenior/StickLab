# StickLab v0.5.13 — Physical ledge climbing

Go Here can now attempt a bounded grip-and-pull over a narrow, fixed, nearly level ledge up to 148 world units tall. The movement planner includes climbable ledges in its ordered route; near the ledge, a resident reaches with its forward hand, attaches a breakable physics hinge to the ledge, and pulls its ragdoll joints upward and forward. The external grip releases when the route advances past the ledge, when a player takes manual control, when movement is cancelled, or when the grip breaks.

The climb uses ordinary simulation constraints and velocity impulses; it does not move a figure directly through a wall. A failed attempt reaches the existing bounded retry and blocked feedback. Carrying while climbing, climbing broad or angled surfaces, and planning vertical routes between platforms are not supported yet.
