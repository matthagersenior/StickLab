# StickLab v0.5.12 — Planned multi-barrier movement

Walk now builds an ordered plan for the fixed barriers between a figure and its destination. Low, narrow barriers that have overhead clearance are queued as physical hop steps. If the straight route contains a wall that the current movement system cannot cross, the figure reports the blockage before attempting the whole trip. The plan refreshes when the destination or relevant obstacle layout changes.

When two residents share a grip, Walk places them on opposite sides of the requested arrival point. A resident that arrives early waits while its partner finishes, keeping the carry coordinated through arrival.

This is destination-wide planning for a straight ground route. It does not find detours, climb walls, navigate vertically between platforms, or guarantee that a physical hop succeeds. A failed hop is still bounded by the existing retry limit and can be corrected by moving the obstacle or choosing another destination.
