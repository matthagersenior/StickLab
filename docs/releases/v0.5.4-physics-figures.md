# StickLab v0.5.4 — Free stick figures for physics

This update fixes the mismatch between the expected sandbox and the default scenes.

## Observed issues
- In v0.5.3 the default Playground deliberately attached a passive figure's **head to a fixed ceiling anchor with a rope**. Walking applies forces but cannot lift its feet off that rope; the figure appeared to hang in place.
- Engineering Lab started with only machines, platforms and boxes. To experiment with a figure there, the player had to know how to scroll to the Ragdoll or Resident tool.
- Imported or locally saved older scenes can still contain tethered figures. Existing Detach can release a rope, but the user had to locate the actual connection or anchor.

## Changes
- **Playground:** the demo passive ragdoll stands unhooked on the ground. A separate autonomous Resident is also present. Rope experiments still exist, attached to a ball rather than a figure.
- **Engineering Lab:** a ground-based autonomous walker and a passive ragdoll now spawn alongside the wheels, motor cart and structures. Figures can be grabbed, dropped, pushed, collide with machine parts, and optionally receive a Walk destination.
- **Physics Figure tool:** formerly labeled Ragdoll; the same physical articulated figure, with its joints and skeleton, can be spawned in any sandbox game including Physics Puzzles.
- **Unhook:** a new action beside Walk in the mobile tool strip and in the side panel releases outside ropes, springs and hinges from the selected or nearest figure without removing its skeleton or the anchors. Undo restores its released connectors.
- **Walk:** explicitly commanding a figure to walk unhooks those external connectors first, then lets gravity settle the character onto the floor and uses its physical locomotion controller. This is an intentional movement action; Unhook alone is available for building experiments.
- Saves remain v4-compatible, including older scenes that still have ropes; nothing is silently rewritten on load.

## Validation
Tests start the Playground and Engineering Lab and verify figures are available and not suspended by default. A legacy imported figure is tethered, unhooked via API, inspected for intact bones/anchor and undo recovery; Walk on the same figure releases its external connection. Browser checks cover a phone-size viewport, the visible Physics Figure and Unhook controls, and a Walk command sent to an Engineering figure.

**Remaining limits:** this is low-cost 2D ragdoll locomotion, not full locomotion robotics. Heavy impacts can topple figures, obstacles can prevent reaching a destination, and a figure suspended far above ground must fall before walking. Complex grappling and multiple attached characters remain an area for improvement.
