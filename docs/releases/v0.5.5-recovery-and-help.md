# StickLab v0.5.5 — Recovering Figures and Helpful Physical Interactions

Living stick figures should try to right themselves after falling and physically influence nearby things, not require the player to control individual joints. Earlier locomotion supported stepping and jumping but no recovery or purposeful push motor.

## Get-up and balance response

- The 2D Verlet ragdoll remains authoritative. Joint positions and collisions are never teleported or disabled.
- The controller notices a collapsed torso (head or chest near/below the hip) and initiates recovery only when joints contact the floor or simple fixed platforms.
- Recovery sends capped upward velocity impulses to the chest/head, weaker impulses to hips/shoulders, and small centering forces.
- Pulses are at least eight physics ticks apart; walking is suspended during recovery to avoid dragging the prone body.
- Automatic residents, manually directed figures and Walk share this controller; passive Physics figures remain passive unless actively controlled or assigned a destination.
- The resident motion-state API reports standing, walking, getting-up and pushing as applicable.

## Physical object assistance

- Resident **Help · push nearby balls** can approach a loose ball, including a physics puzzle target, and apply a forward impulse when close and grounded.
- A small opposing impulse reaches the figure's body. Objects are never teleported and continue to obey gravity, collision and constraints.
- The locomotion actuator can push wheels or unanchored boxes when a valid target is explicitly provided; Help mode currently seeks loose balls.
- An 18-tick push cooldown and local candidate limits bound cost.
- Applying a resident behavior now releases direct control, enables AI and resumes a paused game, so it takes effect immediately.

## Performance and compatibility

The implementation is deterministic and offline, with a constant number of joint impulses and a bounded nearby-body scan per active character. Existing Eco/Standard/High planning caps remain; no new assets, services or packages. Existing v4 scenes remain compatible.

## Verification

- Unit tests: bounded fall recovery on support, no midair thrust, contact-only pushes, no pushes to fixed/distant objects, push cooldown, intent selection.
- Full-simulator tests: fallen figure physically lifts head toward upright over 130 physics steps, Help-mode figure moves an actual ball, Apply Behavior releases direct control.
- Android-sized Chrome browser test: selecting Help in the sidebar triggers autonomous physical movement; portrait/landscape, other game modes, touch, offline build and puzzle tests still run.

**Limitations:** This is an inexpensive active-ragdoll stabilization system, not human biomechanics or guaranteed recovery from every fall. A character pinned by objects or constraints may not recover. There is no full obstacle pathfinding, grasping, lifting, or coordinated manipulation yet; it does not automatically solve physics levels.
