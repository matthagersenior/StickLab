# StickLab v0.3 release

## Integrated gameplay

- Free Sandbox remains unrestricted, with the three original starter worlds.
- Challenge mode adds three physics-based levels, visual goal zones, optional hints, reset/next level, and unlock persistence.
- A level completes only after the designated target ball fits entirely inside its goal area and remains below the movement threshold for 12 consecutive physics ticks. Directly dragging that ball is disabled during unfinished challenges.
- Unlocks do not depend on scene import; importing exits Challenge mode.
- Motor carts have a chassis and two hinged powered wheels; individual wheels can be added and their motors cycled forward/reverse/off.
- Box materials (wood, rubber, steel) have distinct impact durability; damaged non-fixed boxes fracture into bounded debris.
- Newly created ropes, springs, and hinges may break under stress. Migrated legacy links remain non-breakable unless explicitly flagged.

## Format and safety

Scene version 3 stores wheel motor settings, materials/health, and breakable connector strength. Version 1 and 2 worlds remain importable, with a v0.2 save-slot fallback before the v0.1 slot-1 fallback. Validation rejects invalid versions, missing references, duplicate IDs, non-finite positions, malformed motor power, and strength values.

## Performance

Spatial broad-phase hashing and Eco/Standard/High body limits remain. No remote service, game asset download, or account is required. Adaptive FPS measures should be verified on real Android and desktop browsers; physics approximates contact and motor traction.

## Tests

GitHub CI covers generated HTML reproducibility, syntax checks, v1/v2/v3 scene migration, challenge goal containment/dwell, motor behavior, impact damage, connection fracture, mobile cart spawning, and challenges locked until solved.

## Known limitations

Puzzles are open-ended but the initial three are not yet an authored campaign with difficulty ratings or a hint system beyond one hint per level. Motor vehicles use low-cost traction approximations instead of tire friction impulses. Breakage is rudimentary, and unbroken joint strength is not visualized. Autonomous characters and social behavior belong to v0.4.
