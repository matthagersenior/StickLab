# StickLab v0.4.3 — Detach and reusable anchor points

Players can now disconnect construction links without destroying their anchors, figures, or machines.

## Using the tools

1. To **place a new anchor**, choose **Pin** from the horizontal bottom toolbar (beside Ball) or **Anchor** from the ☰ Tools panel, then tap any location in the world. There is no restriction to the original purple anchors.
2. To **connect** bodies, choose **Rope**, **Spring**, or **Hinge** and tap the two parts. Anchors are fixed attachment points; they cannot be dragged while fixed.
3. To **remove one connection**, choose **Detach** (✂) and tap a rope, spring, or hinge near its visible line or hinge point. The objects on either end remain.
4. To **release every connection on an anchor**, use Detach and tap the purple anchor itself. The anchor remains reusable in place.
5. To remove the anchor entirely, use **Erase** and tap it. Erase still removes that object's connections as well.
6. **Undo** can restore the removed links.

All connection changes share one physics scene and can be made while paused or running. The latest viewport fixes for landscape/portrait remain intact. Both the hosted PWA and standalone offline HTML contain the complete tool; the PWA cache version is updated so a page reload picks up the new controls.

## Behavior

- Detach only changes player-made ropes, springs and hinges, not the structural bones or bracing within a stick figure.
- Tap detection is based on the camera zoom so the connection remains a practical touch target at different magnifications.
- Undo preserves the two endpoints and original connector settings.
- Tapping an empty area does not alter the world and gives a hint about what to tap.

## Verification

Tests import multiple anchored links, tap one rope, verify the other link and both endpoints survive, undo, release all links at a fixed anchor, and confirm passive ragdoll skeletons are unaffected. Creating multiple fresh Pin anchors is also covered. The existing phone/tablet/desktop viewport and live rotation checks remain in CI.
