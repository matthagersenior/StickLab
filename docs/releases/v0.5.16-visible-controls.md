# StickLab v0.5.16 — Visible controls and a clearer Living World

The movement pad appears whenever a movable figure is present. Its arrows take control of the nearest figure directly, without requiring a grab or a drag. The **Auto** button releases a resident back to its own behavior. Pressing a movement arrow also resumes a paused simulation.

The get-up motor now reapplies stronger, more frequent torso and head impulses and uses hands or knees as physical braces against the ground. It improves recovery after a fall while keeping the ragdoll under gravity and collision constraints.

Living World now starts residents farther apart with clearer walking lanes and activity props spread across the ground. During a direct Walk, a resident can push a nearby loose ball, box, or unpowered wheel with physical impulses when it blocks the way. Fixed walls, detours, climbing while carrying, and full route planning remain outside this local movement system.
