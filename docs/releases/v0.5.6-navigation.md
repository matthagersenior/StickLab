# StickLab v0.5.6 — Go Here obstacle awareness and stalled-route recovery

The v0.5.5 walking controller already supports physical gait, bounded recovery after falls, and small contact pushes. This release makes destination-following more useful when the world has structures in the way, while preserving full physics interaction.

## New Go Here behaviors

- **Low obstacles:** a nearby, ground-level fixed box approximately knee-high triggers an ordinary grounded hop. The figure's existing gait and jump physics handle motion; the AI does not teleport or reposition limbs.
- **Tall static barriers:** a body-height fixed wall between a figure and its goal produces a **blocked** instruction instead of repeated head-first jumps. The original requested destination is retained.
- **Stalled movement:** after two no-progress checks, the character makes a controlled retry hop. Two unsuccessful retries lead to a blocked state rather than limitless futile forces. Progress of six world units during a planning interval resets the monitor.
- **Directionality:** navigation checks the obstacle face in the actual travel direction, including leftward movement. Overhead platforms and obstacles behind the figure are ignored for these decisions.
- **Live feedback:** a blocked figure displays a warning in the existing on-screen movement label and a single explanatory toast. Choosing Walk and a new destination resets its navigation monitor so it can try again.
- **Existing modes:** Help retains its original jump assistance; Wander can reverse direction when a fixed barrier is too tall. Physics Puzzles, construction, resident behavior, save files, and manual arrow controls still use the existing collision and ragdoll constraints.

## Performance

Route scanning is restricted to the existing 32 nearby AI candidates and runs during the engine's existing slow AI decision ticks. Stuck-state tracking uses a few numbers on active characters. No external libraries, browser APIs, online services, or additional background jobs are needed.

## Verified scenarios

Navigation unit tests cover a low hurdle, too-high wall, left/right barriers, overhead and behind obstacles, bounded retry attempts, progress resets, and changing destinations. Full-game tests cover grounded jump actuation and tall-wall blocked feedback. Android-size Chrome tests verify that a blocked message is visible without overflowing and that choosing a new goal clears the warning. The standard source, offline, PWA, mobile orientation and physics tests are also included in CI.

## Known limitations

This is **local reactive navigation**, not a navigation mesh or guaranteed route planner. It won't automatically detour around complex structures, climb tall walls, manipulate ramps, or reliably traverse multiple elevated platforms. A figure may still fall or get stuck. The blocked warning explicitly tells players to move an obstacle, build another route, or choose a reachable destination.
