# StickLab v0.5.8 — Physical hand grasping

Residents in Help mode can now make simple physical grasps on nearby light objects. The figure does not teleport or attach an object directly to its torso: a low-stiffness spring connects a hand joint to a ball, while a local hinge connects a hand to a small box surface. Gravity, collision, and ragdoll links remain active.

## Behavior

- **Reach and grasp:** residents can hold loose balls up to 42 units in radius and small dynamic boxes up to 72×72 units and a bounded area. The visible connection is made at the actual hand and box surface.
- **Shared hold:** up to two different Help residents can attach separate hands to one light object. Each figure keeps its own physical grip; if either releases, the other connection remains.
- **Release:** changing out of Help mode, disabling Living AI, selecting the figure for manual control, or unhooking its outside connections removes that figure's AI grip. The object remains a normal physical body.
- **Saved scenes:** grasp ownership is retained on the physical link and validated against an active Help resident and a supported light object when scenes load. Older v1–v4 saves remain accepted.

This is a first grasp interaction, not dexterous manipulation. It does not lift heavy boxes, plan a carry route, assign complementary tasks, or guarantee that an object stays held under strong impacts. Complex routes, climbing, richer grasping, and coordinated figure interactions still need development.
