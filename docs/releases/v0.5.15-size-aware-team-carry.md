# StickLab v0.5.15 — Size-aware team carrying

Shared carriers now choose their formation from the held object's collision bounds. Round objects use their radius; boxes use their rotated horizontal extent. Larger objects get more space between helpers, while the shared destination remains centered and is clamped as a unit near either world edge.

The carry stays physical: figures still hold the object through breakable hand connections and move using the existing local movement system. Carrying through climbs, route detours, and coordinated multi-platform travel are still unsupported.
